Privacy Policy

Last updated July 21, 2026 Applies to the Aysippop Store mobile app

In short

  • We collect your email (and your name/photo if you sign in with Google or Facebook) to run your account.
  • Your password is never seen by us — it's handled entirely by Firebase Authentication.
  • Orders are sent as a Messenger chat to the shop, not stored on our servers, and we don't collect payment details in the app.
  • Your theme and favorites stay on your phone — they're never uploaded anywhere.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't run ads or third-party analytics in the app.

01Who we are

A&Y Sippop is a tea & coffee shop located at Rizal St., Bgy. Basud (near Sto. Niño High School). The Aysippop Store app is how our customers browse the menu, build an order, and send it to us to prepare — this policy covers what happens to your information when you use it.

02Information we collect

We keep this to what the app actually needs to run:

What Examples When it's collected
Account details Email address; display name & profile photo (only if you sign in with Google or Facebook) When you register or sign in
Password Encrypted and managed entirely by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store it in readable form When you register with email & password
Order details Items, sizes, quantities, and total When you check out — sent to us as a Messenger message, not stored on our servers
App preferences Theme (light/dark), favorite items Saved only on your device — never leaves your phone

We don't collect payment card numbers, precise location, or contacts through the app.

03How ordering works

Your cart lives only in the app's memory while you're using it — it clears when you close the app, and we don't keep an order history on any server. When you check out, the app formats your order as a text message and opens Facebook Messenger so you can send it directly to our shop's page. From that point, the conversation is between you and A&Y Sippop on Messenger, governed by Meta's own privacy policy for the Messenger platform.

We don't process payment inside the app — payment is arranged with the shop directly, the same way it would be for a phone-in order.

04How we use your information

  • To create and sign you into your account.
  • To let you place an order by handing it off to Messenger.
  • To keep the app working the way you left it (theme, favorites) on your own device.
  • To respond to you if you contact us for support.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

05Third-party services

Signing in and checking out route through a small number of services that process data on our behalf:

Firebase Authentication (Google)

Handles account creation, sign-in, and password storage. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Sign-In

If you choose "Continue with Google," Google shares your name, email, and profile photo with us to create your account.

Facebook Login & Messenger (Meta)

If you choose "Continue with Facebook," Meta shares your name, email, and profile photo with us. Checkout also opens Messenger to send your order. See Meta's Privacy Policy.

06Storage & security

Account data is stored with Firebase Authentication on Google Cloud infrastructure and protected by their standard security practices. Preferences like theme and favorites are stored locally on your device using your phone's app storage and are never transmitted to us. Cart and order-in-progress data exist only in memory while the app is open.

07Your choices & rights

  • Access or correct your details — update your name or photo through whichever provider you signed in with (Google or Facebook), or contact us for anything tied to your account directly.
  • Delete your account — message us (details below) and we'll delete your account record from Firebase Authentication.
  • Clear local data — uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device, including favorites and theme preference.

If you're in the Philippines, these choices are also consistent with your rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

08Children's privacy

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

09Changes to this policy

If how we handle your information changes, we'll update this page and revise the "Last updated" date at the top. Significant changes will be noted in the app.
